More things it can do

By default, your data stays on your computer.

  • All your computers in one viewSync with your own Google Drive or WebDAV, and count the records from all your computers together.
  • Plugins (Focus)Install plugins like a Pomodoro timer in one click, or write your own. Plugin market
  • Plugins (Time Garden)A community plugin made by a user. Your activity time grows flowers. GitHub
  • Custom themesChange the colors, light or dark mode, and page layout. Theme market
  • Got a question? Ask the AI.The AI answers from your records. By default, it uses a local AI.
  • AI writes your daily and weekly reportsSum up the day by time of day, choose which app categories to include, and export the result.
  • Search the text on your screenFind any text that was on your screen.
  • Custom activity categoriesChoose the category for each app, for clearer stats.
