【0.8.24】
- ⚠️ App categories no longer sync to versions older than 0.7.0. If you use several devices, make sure every one of them is updated (as announced in 0.8.23)
- ⚠️ After updating, do not go back to 0.8.22 or earlier: assigning a category to an app fails on those versions
- Saving a group or a category without changing anything no longer overwrites real edits made on another device
